ShopEmpire Review 2026 – What Is It?

Promoting a trending product as an affiliate looks simple until you reach the video. You have to find something that is selling, make footage of it, write a caption, add your link and post it again on each platform. Many people stall at the video, either because of the cost or because they do not want to appear on camera.

An AI tool can shorten that work, but the choices still matter. Picking the niche, judging which products are worth promoting and posting consistently remain your job.

ShopEmpire puts product discovery, an AI video builder, an affiliate-link caption and a multi-platform scheduler into one app, so you can go from a niche to a published video in fewer steps.

WHAT DOES IT COVER?

ShopEmpire is designed to turn a real product photo into a short promotional video without you appearing in it. You build a video in 4 steps:

  • Step 1: Find a trending product
    Pick a source (TikTok Shop, Amazon or eBay), a niche and a target market, and see what is selling with live prices and discounts.
  • Step 2: Attach your affiliate link
    Save products into a campaign, each holding its own affiliate link.
  • Step 3: Pick a template and let the assistant build
    The AI Studio Assistant lays the product images into the template's scenes and shows you the plan before anything is generated or charged.
  • Step 4: Publish
    Publish now or schedule to TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest with your link in the caption.

ShopEmpire can be useful for affiliate marketers, content creators, store owners and dropshippers who want video for their own catalogue, and freelancers or small agencies who want to offer product video to local businesses under the commercial license.

To sum up, ShopEmpire offers a practical way to remove the filming, editing and on-camera parts of product video. You still need to choose products carefully, follow each marketplace's and platform's rules, and keep posting.

Features are described as the vendor presents them. The vendor does not state exactly which marketplace categories or countries are supported at any given time, so check the official page for current coverage.

ShopEmpire Review 2026 – How Does It Work?

  • Choose a source (TikTok Shop, Amazon or eBay), a niche and a target market.
  • Review the trending products it returns and save the ones you want as a campaign.
  • Attach your own affiliate link to each product. You need an approved affiliate account for the marketplace you use.
  • Pick a template and let the assistant lay the product images into its scenes.
  • Review the plan, adjust scenes, images and lengths, then approve it. Credits are spent when the video is generated.
  • Check the finished video, then publish or schedule it to your connected accounts, following each platform's rules for AI-generated content and affiliate disclosure.

Who Is ShopEmpire For?

It may be a fit if you are:

  • An affiliate marketer or content creator who wants product video without filming or appearing on camera.
  • A store owner, dropshipper or boutique seller who wants video for your own catalogue.

  • Expect a specific income by a specific date. No tool can promise that.
  • Do not want to apply for affiliate programs, which need approval, or follow marketplace and platform rules.
  • Do not want to manage a credit balance, since videos use credits.

Before You Buy: Set Realistic Expectations

  • No guarantee of income. The vendor's disclaimer says the product does not guarantee income or success. Affiliate earnings depend on the products you choose, how often you post and your market.
  • Affiliate approval is required. Amazon, eBay and TikTok Shop each have their own affiliate programs and rules. Read and follow them.
  • Understand the credits. The app is a one-time payment, but each video uses credits. Work out how many videos you expect to make and what top-ups cost.
  • Check platform rules. Social platforms have their own policies on AI-generated content and require affiliate links to be disclosed. Read them before you publish, and disclose your affiliate relationship.
  • Review every video. Check that the finished video matches the real product and that its claims are accurate before it goes live.
  • Use client work responsibly. Only use photos you have the right to use, and check what a client's photos and branding permit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ShopEmpire?

It is a faceless AI product-video tool. It surfaces trending products from TikTok Shop, Amazon and eBay, builds a video from the seller's product photo, adds your affiliate link to the caption and publishes to five social platforms.

Do I need to show my face or use my voice?

The vendor says no. The product is what appears on screen, and you are never in the video.

Do I need to buy the products I promote?

No. You promote them as an affiliate, and the video is built from the seller's own product photo.

How do I get paid?

Through the affiliate program of the marketplace you promote, at that program's rates. You need an approved affiliate account with it. ShopEmpire does not pay you.

Is there a monthly fee?

The vendor describes the app as a one-time payment with no monthly platform fee. Generating videos uses credits, and top-up packs are sold separately.

How many videos can I make with 10,000 credits?

The vendor's FAQ estimates about 7 to 19 complete videos, depending on whether you make short single-clip spotlights or longer multi-scene videos.

Can I use it for client work?

Yes. The vendor says the commercial license is included, so you can make videos for clients and keep what you charge.

Which platforms can it publish to?

TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Pinterest. You connect your own accounts.

Will the videos look fake?

The vendor says the video is built from the real product photo, with the AI handling lighting, scene and camera motion. Judge the results yourself before you publish, because quality can vary by product and template.

Does it work outside the US?

The vendor says you can source products from TikTok Shop in 16 countries, plus Amazon and eBay, and publish globally. Check the official page for current coverage.

Do I need the upgrades?

No. The core product works on its own. See the upgrades section above and read each offer before you add it.

Are there bonuses?

Yes. The vendor lists four launch bonuses with the core product and four more with the bundle. They come from the vendor, not from AiReview.

Is there a refund?

The vendor states a 14-day satisfaction guarantee. You contact the vendor within 14 days. Refunds are handled by the vendor.
